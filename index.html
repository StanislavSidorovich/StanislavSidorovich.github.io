01 · About

Between the site and the management meeting

For most of my career I've been the person in the middle: collecting numbers from contractors and colleagues, checking them against what is really happening on site, and writing it up so management knows what happened, why, and what needs a decision. At Badra I saw how it looks when it works: everyone knew their job, and the head of department never left you alone with a hard problem. That's the kind of team I try to be part of.

After twelve years I decided to change country and field at once. My brother lives in Amsterdam, my sister has just started at UCLL in Belgium, and I came to Lisbon for the MSc at NOVA IMS. It joins BI with data mining, process mining and business process management, which is the analytical side of what I did on projects for years. Now I want to do it properly.

Lead Reporting Specialist

EuroChem Karatau · chemical complex project, Kazakhstan

The weekly report grew with the project: more metrics, more milestones, then commissioning, and every stage brought new people to collect data from. It never slipped past its reporting week, even when colleagues left without notice or were on trips to China and I had to reach them by phone. When something was late, I took it on myself rather than blame a colleague. Head office taught me to check numbers more carefully, and a few PowerPoint tricks; Excel formulas did the rest.

Planning & Reporting Manager

GazpromNeft Business Service · Badra oilfield, Iraq (USD 2bn+)

Daily to monthly reporting on a ~USD 80M infrastructure scope with two main contractors. My part went to the central office in Dubai, where it joined wells, pipelines, engineering and long-lead equipment in the report for partner meetings.

Colleagues and contractors came from many countries, and most contractors genuinely wanted to finish on time, so much of the job was sitting down with them to rework schedules and agree priorities. Site visits meant body armour and an armoured car. When one main contractor pulled out after underbidding, I was moved to support another scope.
