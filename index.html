01 · About

Between the site and the management meeting

For most of my career I've been the person in the middle: collecting numbers from contractors and colleagues, checking them against what is really happening on site, and writing it up so management knows what happened, why, and what needs a decision. At Badra I saw how it looks when it works: everyone knew their job, and the head of department never left you alone with a hard problem. That's the kind of team I try to be part of.

The turn towards data started before Lisbon. My brother, a tech team lead in Amsterdam who has worked alongside analysts for years, suggested I try it. In 2024 I took the Google Data Analytics course, in 2025 Business Analysis with Power BI at Udacity, then SQL and DAX, and built Quaera along the way. Most of what I build now, I build with AI agents: I set the task, check the result and steer, and they write the code. That is how Quaera and this site were made.

After twelve years I decided to change country and field at once. My sister has just started at UCLL in Belgium, and I came to Lisbon for the MSc at NOVA IMS. It joins BI with data mining, process mining and business process management, which is the analytical side of what I did on projects for years. Now I want to do it properly.

Lead Reporting Specialist

The weekly report grew with the project: more metrics, more milestones, then commissioning, and every stage brought new people to collect and check data from, so the report showed what was really happening on site. It didn't always land on time. When something was late, I took it on myself rather than blame a colleague. Head office taught me to check numbers more carefully, and a few PowerPoint tricks; Excel formulas did the rest.
